What you get

  • A tide chart for today, with a line showing the current time
  • High and low tides marked on the chart. Hover or tap a point for its time and height
  • The current water level in feet
  • Times shown for the station's location, not your own time zone
  • Predictions from NOAA for tide stations on U.S. coasts and territories

How it works

  1. Open the app and choose Use My Location to see tide stations near you, or click the map to pick a spot.
  2. When you click the map, FlowEbb shows the tide for the nearest NOAA tide station. You can also click any station marker.
  3. Read the current tide and today's chart. Your last spot is remembered in your browser for next time.

Common questions

Is FlowEbb free?

Yes. FlowEbb is free to use and has no ads. You do not need to create an account to see tides.

Where does the tide data come from?

Tide predictions come from NOAA CO-OPS, the Center for Operational Oceanographic Products and Services. FlowEbb shows the predictions for the station nearest the spot you choose.

Which places are covered?

FlowEbb covers the tide prediction stations that NOAA publishes, which are mostly along U.S. coasts and in U.S. territories. If you pick a spot far from any of them, the nearest station may be a long way away, so check the station name shown above the chart.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. FlowEbb runs in the web browser on phones, tablets, and computers, with nothing to install. Choose Use My Location to see stations near you, or tap the map.
